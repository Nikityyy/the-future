From Bitpanda to your phone.

There is no sign-in with Bitpanda and no server in between. GitHub, which you already control, does the reading.

  1. Create a read-only key

    In Bitpanda, under Account and API keys, give the key the Read scope only. Without Trade it cannot buy, sell or withdraw anything. You can delete it at any time.

  2. Put it in your own repository

    Fork the project on GitHub and add two secrets: the key, and a long passphrase of your own. GitHub reads your portfolio every three hours, seals it with the passphrase and publishes only the sealed file.

  3. Open the app and enter the passphrase

    The app opens the sealed file on your phone, then asks for a six digit PIN. Turn on Face ID in the app and even the PIN stays in your pocket.

Private, and you can check it.

  • The key can only read

    Bitpanda keys have scopes. This one has the Read scope and nothing else.

  • The key never reaches your phone

    It lives in a GitHub secret. Your phone only ever receives a sealed file, locked with AES-256 and a key stretched from your passphrase 600,000 times.

  • A PIN or your face guards the app

    The passphrase is sealed on your phone under your PIN, or under a passkey that Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint unlocks. The app locks itself two minutes after you leave it.

  • Open source, no tracking

    No account, no analytics, no ads. Read every line on GitHub.

The sealed file is public, as GitHub Pages is. Only your passphrase opens it, so choose four random words rather than a name or a date.

Keep it on your home screen.

It is a web app, so there is nothing to download from a store. Installed, it opens full screen, works offline and feels like any other app.

iPhone

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from your home screen.

Android and computer

  1. Open the app in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Choose Install app from the menu or the address bar.
  3. Open it like any other app.
